# Le français de A à Z — 12-Month French School

**Designed for:** An adult with a day job, starting from zero, who wants to *speak*, not just pass quizzes. It suits visual learners who need repetition.
**Goal:** Solid **B1** (independent user) in 12 months, ready to sit **DELF B1**, with a clear Year 2 path to **B2 / NCLC 7** on **TCF Canada / TEF Canada**.
**Time:** ~1 hour a day, Monday–Saturday, plus a light Sunday (30–60 min). About **360–400 hours** in the year, which is what Alliance Française estimates for B1.

> **Why French matters for Canada:** NCLC 7 in all four French skills gives up to **+50 CRS points** in Express Entry (with CLB 5+ English) and makes you eligible for the **French-language proficiency category draws**, which have invited candidates with much lower scores than general draws. NCLC 7 ≈ B2. This year gets you to B1; Year 2 (outlined at the bottom) gets you to NCLC 7.

## How This Course Works

### Daily Structure (Mon–Sat, ~60 min)
| Block | Time | What You Do |
|---|---|---|
| Review | 10 min | Anki flashcards (every day, no exceptions) + re-read yesterday's notes out loud. |
| Lesson | 20 min | One grammar point or vocabulary set: watch the video, read the explanation, copy the examples by hand. |
| Practice | 15 min | Written exercises. Check with the answer key. Write your mistakes in your error log. |
| Speak & Listen | 10 min | Shadow audio, record yourself, or talk. **Always out loud.** |
| Log | 5 min | 3 sentences **in French** (as soon as you can): what I learned, what confused me, what I'll review tomorrow. |

**Saturday** is a project day: a longer speaking or writing task using the whole week.
**Sunday:** Rest + 30–60 min of *enjoyable* French: an Easy French video, a podcast, a French song with lyrics. Read your week's log. No grammar.

### Your Learning Rules
1. **Speak from Day 1.** Out loud, every day, even alone. Your mouth has to learn French too.
2. **Anki every day.** 10 minutes of spaced repetition beats 2 hours of cramming.
3. **Learn words in sentences.** Not *manger = to eat*, but *Je mange du riz.*
4. **Mistakes go in the error log.** Re-read it every Sunday. Your mistakes are your personal curriculum.
5. **Listen more than feels necessary.** Understanding comes before speaking. Always have French in your ears.
6. **Gender is part of the word.** Never learn *table*; learn *une table*.

### Tools to Set Up in Week 1
- **Anki** (free): one deck called *Français*. Add ~10 cards a day from the lesson (French sentence on the front, English plus audio on the back).
- **A notebook** with three sections: *Leçons*, *Mes erreurs* (error log), *Journal* (daily log).
- **Voice recorder** on your phone. You'll record yourself every week and compare with Week 1.
- **Free accounts:** TV5MONDE Apprendre, Kwiziq French, Tandem (conversation exchange).

## Full Syllabus — Week by Week

### Phase 1 · A1.1 — Les bases
| Week | Title | Grammar | Vocabulary & functions | By Saturday you can… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bonjour ! | Alphabet, accents, être (je/tu/il/elle) | Greetings, politeness, numbers 0–10, *je m'appelle* | Greet, say your name, spell it |
| 2 | Qui suis-je ? | Être (all), gender of adjectives, questions with intonation & *est-ce que* | Nationalities, countries, languages, numbers 11–20 | Introduce yourself fully |
| 3 | Les choses autour de moi | Gender of nouns, un/une/des, le/la/les/l', plurals, *il y a*, *c'est* | Objects, classroom/office, numbers 21–69 | Describe what's in a room |
| 4 | Ma famille | Avoir, possessives (mon/ma/mes…), adjective agreement | Family, age, numbers 70–100 | Present your family |
| 5 | J'aime, je n'aime pas | Present of -ER verbs, negation *ne…pas* | Jobs, hobbies, likes & dislikes | Talk about your work and hobbies |
| 6 | Ma journée | Pronominal verbs (*se lever*), *faire*, time | Time, days, months, dates, routine | Describe your daily routine |
| 7 | À table ! | Partitive articles (du/de la/des, pas de), *vouloir*, *prendre* | Food, drinks, quantities, prices, restaurant | Order food and shop at a market |
| 8 | Révision & milestone | Aller + à (au/à la/aux), review of Weeks 1–7 | Places in town | Record a 2-minute self-introduction |

## Assessment
- **Weekly quiz:** a self-check on Saturday evening. Pass = you can answer everything without notes. If you can't, repeat the week's Saturday before moving on.
- **Phase milestones:** see the table above. Mock exams use the official DELF sample papers (free, with answer keys).
- **Recording diary:** record the same 1-minute self-introduction in Weeks 1, 8, 17, 26, 34, 43 and 52. Listening back is the best motivation there is.

## Year 2 Preview — B2 / NCLC 7 (Weeks 53–78)
| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 53–58 | B2 grammar: advanced subjunctive, concession (*quoique, bien que*), past conditional, participle clauses |
| 59–64 | Listening at native speed: Radio-Canada, France Culture, podcasts; note-taking |
| 65–70 | Argued writing at B2 (250 words); formal register; TCF/TEF writing tasks |
| 71–74 | Speaking: TCF/TEF speaking tasks; weekly tutor sessions |
| 75–78 | Full TCF Canada / TEF Canada mocks → sit the real exam, target **NCLC 7 in all four skills** |

*TCF Canada NCLC 7 thresholds: Listening 458+, Reading 453+, Writing 10/20, Speaking 10/20. Your NCLC is your **lowest** skill, so no skill can be neglected.*
