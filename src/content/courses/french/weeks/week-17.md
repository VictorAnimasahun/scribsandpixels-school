## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** Listening and reading strategies
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Listening strategies**
1. **Read the questions before the audio.** Underline what you need: a time? a place? a price?
2. First listening: the general idea. Second listening: the details.
3. Numbers and times are always tested. Revise 70–99 and the 24-hour clock.
4. Never leave a multiple-choice question blank.

**Reading strategies**
1. Look at the *type* of document first (ad, email, sign, schedule).
2. Scan for the keywords from the question; you don't need every word.
3. Answers are often paraphrased: *le magasin est fermé le dimanche* ↔ *on ne peut pas y aller le dimanche*.

### Practice (15 min)
2 A1 reading exercises on TV5MONDE, timed. Apply the strategies.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
2 A1 listening exercises on TV5MONDE, applying the strategies.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Writing practice
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Task 1: the form.** Practise filling in: nom, prénom, date de naissance, nationalité, adresse, profession, loisirs, raison du voyage. Write dates as *15/03/1992*.

**Task 2: the message (40–50 words).** A checklist:
- ✅ Greeting (*Salut Marie, / Chère Marie,*) and sign-off (*À bientôt ! Bises, …*)
- ✅ Answer **every** point in the instructions
- ✅ At least 40 words (count them!)
- ✅ Simple, correct sentences beat long, broken ones
- ✅ Re-read for agreement (e/s) and verb endings

### Practice (15 min)
Write two messages, timed at 12 minutes each:
1. You're on holiday in Dakar. Write a postcard to a friend: where you are, the weather, what you do, what you eat.
2. Invite a colleague to your birthday party: day, time, place, what to bring.
Then count the words and check with the checklist.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Read both messages aloud.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Speaking practice
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Part 1: Entretien dirigé** (~1 min): the examiner asks about you. Have ready: name, age, nationality, city, family, job, hobbies, routine, why French.
**Part 2: Échange d'informations** (~2 min): you get word cards (*profession ?, week-end ?, sport ?, famille ?*) and must **ask the examiner questions**: *Qu'est-ce que vous faites comme profession ? Qu'est-ce que vous faites le week-end ?*
**Part 3: Dialogue simulé** (~2 min): a role-play buying something with picture-cards of objects and prices: greet, ask for items, ask prices, pay, thank.

### Practice (15 min)
Record all three parts:
1. Answer 8 personal questions without notes.
2. Ask 6 questions from these words: *famille, travail, sport, vacances, cuisine, ville*.
3. Buy 3 items in a shop (a bag €25, sunglasses €15, a hat €12): ask, compare, pay.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Listen back to your recording. Note 3 mistakes in *Mes erreurs*.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 6 — Saturday
**Topic:** Milestone: full DELF A1 mock exam
**Time:** ~2 hours

### Review (10 min)

### Weekly Project (90 min)
**Sit the DELF A1 sample paper under real conditions:**
1. Listening (~20 min): play each recording twice, no pausing.
2. Reading (30 min).
3. Writing (30 min).
4. Speaking: record yourself doing all three parts (prepare 10 min with the task cards).

**Mark it** with the official answer key and marking grids. For the speaking and writing, be honest or ask a Tandem partner to grade you.

### Log (10 min)
Write your scores: Listening /25, Reading /25, Writing /25, Speaking /25, Total /100.

- **Below 50:** repeat the weakest skill's practice day next week before Week 18. That's normal and fine.
