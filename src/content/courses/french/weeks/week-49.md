## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** Listening strategies at B1
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
1. **Use the reading time before the audio** to underline question keywords and predict answers.
2. **First listening**: global understanding (who? where? topic? tone?). **Second**: details.
3. **Numbers, dates, prices and places** are always tested. Write them down immediately.
4. **Distractors:** the audio often *mentions* wrong options. Listen for negation and correction (*non, pas celui-là, finalement…*).
5. **Tone and intention** questions (*Quel est l'avis de… ?*): listen for opinion words (*malheureusement, heureusement, je regrette, c'est génial*).
6. In MCQ exams (TCF): **never leave a blank**, and don't get stuck; move on and come back.

### Practice (15 min)
2 B1 listening exercises on TV5MONDE, applying all 6 strategies. Note which one helped most.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Listen to one Journal en français facile item and write 5 facts (numbers, names, places).

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Reading strategies at B1
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
1. **Read the questions first**, then the text.
2. **Skim** (title, first and last paragraphs) for the main idea; **scan** for specific answers.
3. **Paraphrase alert:** the correct answer rarely uses the same words as the text.
4. **Vrai / Faux + justification**: copy the **exact** phrase that proves it, and make sure it really proves it.
5. **Unknown words:** use the context, word families (*chômage → chômeur*), and cognates (*nécessaire*).
6. **Time management**: DELF B1 reading has 45 minutes. Spend ~20 on each main document and keep 5 to check.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** TCF Canada writing and speaking tasks
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**TCF Canada writing (60 minutes):**
- Task 1 (60–120 words): a message to describe or explain (*Écrivez un message à un ami pour l'inviter…*)
- Task 2 (120–150 words): recount an experience and comment on it (a blog post, an article)
- Task 3 (120–180 words): compare two points of view from 2 short documents, then give yours

**TCF Canada speaking (~12 minutes):**
- Task 1 (~2 min): guided interview about yourself
- Task 2 (~5.5 min, 2 min prep): ask the examiner questions to get information in a situation
- Task 3 (~4.5 min): express and defend your opinion on a question

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Exam day logistics and mindset
**Time:** ~45 min

### Review (15 min)
Anki. Re-read your "Top 10 writing rules" (Week 47).

### Lesson (15 min)
**The day before:** no new material, light listening only, prepare ID and convocation, sleep.
**On the day:** arrive early, eat, bring water and a watch (no phone), read every instruction twice, keep an eye on the time.
**When you panic:** breathe, skip the question, come back. One hard question never decides the result.
**Mindset:** you've studied for ~48 weeks. Trust the work.
