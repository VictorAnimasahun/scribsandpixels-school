## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** The DELF A2 format + grammar review
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Re-read all of *Mes erreurs* since Week 18.

### Lesson (20 min)
**DELF A2: /100, pass = 50 with at least 5/25 per skill.**
| Part | Time | Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Compréhension de l'oral | ~25 min | 4–5 recordings (announcements, radio, dialogues), heard twice |
| Compréhension des écrits | 30 min | 4–5 documents (emails, ads, instructions, short articles) |
| Production écrite | 45 min | 2 texts of **60–80 words**: 1. describe an event or experience; 2. a letter/email (invite, thank, apologise, ask, inform…) |
| Production orale | 6–8 min + 10 min prep | 1. *entretien dirigé* (about you); 2. *monologue suivi* (talk about a topic from your life); 3. *exercice en interaction* (role-play: solve a practical situation) |

**Grammar checklist:** passé composé vs imparfait · futur simple · conditionnel (politeness) · COD/COI · *y/en* · *qui/que/où* · comparatives · negatives · the reflexive verbs. Re-read your one-page summaries.

## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** Listening and reading at A2
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
A2 differences from A1: longer recordings, more **implicit** information, and **true / false + justify** questions in reading (you must copy the sentence that proves your answer).
Strategy for "vrai/faux/justifiez": find the paragraph, copy the **exact** words, and check that your quote really proves the point.

### Practice (15 min)
2 A2 reading exercises on TV5MONDE, timed.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
2 A2 listening exercises; for each, write a one-sentence summary.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Writing at A2
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Task 1: an experience (60–80 words)**, e.g. *Racontez votre dernier week-end / un voyage / une fête.* Check: past tenses correct (PC for events, imparfait for description), sequencing words, feelings.
**Task 2: a message**, e.g. *Vous ne pouvez pas aller à l'anniversaire d'un ami : vous vous excusez, expliquez pourquoi et proposez autre chose.* Check: the right register (tu/vous), **all** requested points, greeting and closing.
Counting: 60–80 words. Under 54 costs points.

### Practice (15 min)
Write both, timed at 20 minutes each:
1. *Racontez une journée où tout est allé de travers.* (a day when everything went wrong)
2. *Vous remerciez un collègue français qui vous a aidé pendant votre installation, et vous l'invitez à dîner.*

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Read both texts aloud and correct them with your checklist.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Speaking at A2
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Monologue suivi** (~2 min): typical topics are *votre ville, votre travail, vos loisirs, votre meilleur ami, vos vacances, un repas de fête*. Structure: introduction → 3 points with examples → personal feeling.
**Exercice en interaction** (~3 min): a practical situation, e.g. *Vous êtes dans un magasin et vous voulez échanger un vêtement* or *Vous organisez une sortie avec un ami*. Greet, explain, ask questions, react, find a solution, thank. Use the conditional for politeness!

### Practice (15 min)
Record:
1. A 2-minute monologue: *Parlez de votre ville.*
2. A 3-minute role-play (play both sides): returning a faulty item and asking for an exchange.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Listen back. Note 3 mistakes and re-record one section.

### Log (5 min)
Log.
