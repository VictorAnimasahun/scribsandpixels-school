## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** Masculine and feminine nouns
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Introduce yourself out loud (last week's project) without notes.

### Lesson (20 min)
Every French noun is **masculine** or **feminine**, even objects. There's no logic (*une table*, *un bureau*), so **always learn a noun with its article.**

The endings help, and they're right ~80% of the time:
| Usually feminine | Usually masculine |
|---|---|
| **-tion / -sion**: la nation, la télévision | **-ment**: le moment, le gouvernement |
| **-té**: la liberté, l'université | **-age**: le fromage, le voyage (but *la page, la plage, l'image*) |
| **-ure**: la voiture, la culture | **-eau**: le bureau, le gâteau (but *l'eau* is feminine) |
| **-ette**: la baguette, la cigarette | **-isme**: le tourisme |
| **-ence / -ance**: la science, la chance | most words ending in a consonant: le lit, le sac |
| **-ie**: la boulangerie, la vie | |

**Starting this week, your log is in French**, using these fixed phrases:
- *Aujourd'hui : …* (Today: [topic])
- *Difficile : …* (Hard: …)
- *Demain, je révise : …* (Tomorrow I review: …)

### Practice (15 min)
Guess the gender from the ending, then check in WordReference:
1. information  2. fromage  3. télévision  4. bâtiment  5. voiture  6. château  7. identité  8. racisme

**Check:** f, m, f, m, f, m, f, m

- Say the 8 words above with *le* or *la* 3 times each.

### Log (5 min)
Log (in French, using the fixed phrases).

## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** un, une, des + everyday objects
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Name 5 feminine and 5 masculine endings.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Indefinite articles** (a / some):
| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | **un** livre | **une** table |
| Plural | **des** livres | **des** tables |

*Des* has no English equivalent. English says "books", French must say *des livres*.

**Objects** (make Anki cards for all of them, with the article):

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** le, la, l', les + plurals
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Point at 10 objects and name them with *un/une*.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Definite articles** (the):
| | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | **le** livre | **la** table |
| Before a vowel or silent h | **l'**ordinateur | **l'**école |
| Plural | **les** livres | **les** tables |

When to use them:
- A specific thing: *Le livre est sur la table.* (The book is on the table.)
- **Things in general** (where English uses no article): *J'aime le café.* (I like coffee.) *La vie est belle.* (Life is beautiful.)

**Plurals**
- Most nouns add **-s**, which is *silent*, so the article tells you it's plural: *le livre / les livres* sound the same except for le/les.
- **-eau → -eaux**: un bureau → des bureaux · **-al → -aux**: un journal → des journaux
- Words ending in **-s, -x, -z** don't change: un bus → des bus, un prix → des prix

### Practice (15 min)
Make these plural:
1. le stylo  2. la clé  3. l'ordinateur  4. le bureau  5. le journal  6. le bus

**Check:** 1. les stylos 2. les clés 3. les ordinateurs (liaison: *les_ordinateurs*) 4. les bureaux 5. les journaux 6. les bus

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Il y a, c'est, and where things are
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Plural of: *le château, l'hôpital, le gâteau* (les châteaux, les hôpitaux, les gâteaux).

### Lesson (20 min)
- **Il y a** = there is / there are (never changes): *Il y a un ordinateur. Il y a des livres.*
- **Qu'est-ce que c'est ?** = What is it? → **C'est** un stylo. / **Ce sont** des clés.
- **Où est… ?** = Where is…? → *Le téléphone est **sur** la table.*

| Preposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| sur | on |
| sous | under |
| dans | in |
| devant | in front of |
| derrière | behind |
| à côté de | next to |

*À côté de* + *le* becomes **du**: *à côté du lit*. (You'll see this *de + le = du* rule again.)

### Practice (15 min)
Translate:
1. There is a bag under the chair.
2. What is it? It's a key.
3. The glasses are in the bag.
4. There are books on the desk.

**Check:** 1. *Il y a un sac sous la chaise.* 2. *Qu'est-ce que c'est ? C'est une clé.* 3. *Les lunettes sont dans le sac.* 4. *Il y a des livres sur le bureau.*

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
- Language Transfer tracks 27–28.
- Hide an object and describe where it is out loud: *Le stylo est sous le cahier.*

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Numbers 21–69
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Numbers 0–20, fast.

### Lesson (20 min)
| Tens | | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| 20 vingt | 21 vingt **et un**, 22 vingt-deux, 23 vingt-trois… | *et un* for 21, 31, 41, 51, 61 |
| 30 trente | 31 trente et un, 32 trente-deux… | then hyphens: *trente-deux* |
| 40 quarante | 41 quarante et un… | |
| 50 cinquante | 51 cinquante et un… | |
| 60 soixante | 61 soixante et un… 69 soixante-neuf | (70+ is strange; that's next week!) |

In *vingt-deux*, the **t of vingt is pronounced** ("vant-deuh"), unlike *vingt* alone.

### Practice (15 min)
1. Write in words: 24, 31, 38, 45, 57, 66.
2. Write in figures: *quarante-trois, soixante et un, cinquante-neuf, trente-sept*.

**Check:** (1) vingt-quatre, trente et un, trente-huit, quarante-cinq, cinquante-sept, soixante-six (2) 43, 61, 59, 37

## Day 6 — Saturday
**Topic:** Project day: label your world
**Time:** ~1.5 hours

### Review (15 min)

### Weekly Project (45 min)
**1. Label your home.** Write 20 sticky notes with French names and articles (*la porte, le frigo, la fenêtre, le miroir*…) and stick them on the objects. Leave them up for a month.

**2. Describe your room** in 8–10 sentences, using *il y a*, articles and prepositions. Model:
> Dans ma chambre, il y a un lit, une table et deux chaises. L'ordinateur est sur la table. Il y a des livres sous le lit. La lampe est à côté du lit. Il n'y a pas de télévision.

(*Il n'y a pas de…* = there isn't any…. It's a preview; just learn it as a phrase.)

## Quiz
1. Give 3 feminine and 3 masculine noun endings.
2. What's the difference between *un livre* and *le livre*?
3. How do you say "I like tea" (tea = *thé*, masculine)?
4. Plural of *le bureau*, *le journal*, *l'ami*.
5. Translate: "There are two keys on the table."
6. How do you say 21, 41 and 68?
7. Name 10 objects in your room with their articles.

**Answers:** (1) f: -tion, -té, -ure, -ette, -ence; m: -ment, -age, -eau, -isme (2) "a book" vs "the book" (3) *J'aime le thé.* (general things take *le/la/les*) (4) les bureaux, les journaux, les amis (5) *Il y a deux clés sur la table.* (6) vingt et un, quarante et un, soixante-huit (7) check each gender in WordReference.
