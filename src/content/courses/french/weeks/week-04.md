## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** Avoir (to have) and its expressions
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Describe your room out loud (last week's project).

### Lesson (20 min)
| Singular | | Plural | |
|---|---|---|---|
| j'ai | I have | nous avons | we have |
| tu as | you have | vous avez | you have |
| il / elle / on a | he / she has | ils / elles ont | they have |

⚠️ **ils_ont** (liaison, a "z" sound: "eel-zon") = they have, but **ils sont** ("eel-son") = they are. Listen carefully; this pair confuses everyone.

French uses *avoir* where English uses "to be":
| French | English |
|---|---|
| J'ai 32 ans. | I am 32 (years old). |
| J'ai faim / soif. | I'm hungry / thirsty. |
| J'ai chaud / froid. | I'm hot / cold. |
| J'ai peur. | I'm scared. |
| J'ai raison / tort. | I'm right / wrong. |
| J'ai besoin de… | I need… |

### Practice (15 min)
Fill in the form of *avoir*:
1. Nous ___ deux enfants.  2. Tu ___ quel âge ?  3. Ils ___ faim.  4. J'___ besoin d'un café.  5. Vous ___ raison.  6. Elle ___ 25 ans.

**Check:** 1. avons 2. as 3. ont 4. ai 5. avez 6. a

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
- Language Transfer tracks 31–32.
- Say *ils ont / ils sont* 10 times, alternating, and exaggerate the z and s sounds.

### Log (5 min)
Log in French: *Aujourd'hui : … / Difficile : … / Demain, je révise : …*

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Possessive adjectives: mon, ma, mes…
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Describe your immediate family in 4 sentences.

### Lesson (20 min)
| Owner | Masc. singular | Fem. singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| my | mon père | ma mère | mes parents |
| your (tu) | ton frère | ta sœur | tes frères |
| his / her | son oncle | sa tante | ses cousins |
| our | notre fils | notre fille | nos enfants |
| your (vous) | votre mari | votre femme | vos parents |
| their | leur père | leur mère | leurs enfants |

Two rules that surprise English speakers:
1. **The possessive agrees with the thing owned, not the owner.** *Sa mère* = his mother OR her mother. *Son frère* = his brother OR her brother.
2. **Before a feminine word starting with a vowel, use mon/ton/son**: *mon amie*, *ton école*, *son histoire*. It's easier to say.

### Practice (15 min)
Choose the possessive:
1. (my) ___ sœur  2. (my) ___ amie  3. (his) ___ voiture  4. (her) ___ père  5. (our) ___ enfants  6. (their) ___ maison  7. (your, vous) ___ parents

**Check:** 1. ma 2. mon 3. sa 4. son 5. nos 6. leur 7. vos

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
- Language Transfer tracks 35–36.
- Coffee Break French lesson 9.

### Log (5 min)

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Numbers 70–100 (and ages)
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Numbers 21–69: count by tens and say 5 random numbers.

### Lesson (20 min)
From 70, French counts in twenties:
| Number | French | Literally |
|---|---|---|
| 70 | soixante-dix | sixty-ten |
| 71 | soixante et onze | sixty and eleven |
| 72 | soixante-douze | sixty-twelve |
| 79 | soixante-dix-neuf | sixty-ten-nine |
| 80 | quatre-vingts | four twenties |
| 81 | quatre-vingt-un | four-twenty-one (no *et*, no *s*) |
| 90 | quatre-vingt-dix | four-twenty-ten |
| 99 | quatre-vingt-dix-neuf | four-twenty-ten-nine |
| 100 | cent | |

In Belgium and Switzerland: *septante* (70), *nonante* (90), and in Switzerland *huitante* (80). Canada uses the France system.

Ages: *Quel âge as-tu ? / Quel âge avez-vous ?* → *J'ai trente-quatre ans.*

### Practice (15 min)
1. Write in words: 70, 75, 80, 84, 91, 97.
2. Write in figures: *soixante-treize, quatre-vingt-deux, quatre-vingt-seize*.

**Check:** (1) soixante-dix, soixante-quinze, quatre-vingts, quatre-vingt-quatre, quatre-vingt-onze, quatre-vingt-dix-sept (2) 73, 82, 96

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
- Language Transfer tracks 37–38.
- Say the ages of 5 family members out loud.

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Describing people: adjective agreement
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Count by tens from 10 to 100.

### Lesson (20 min)
*Il/Elle est comment ?* (What is he/she like?)
| Masculine | Feminine | |
|---|---|---|
| grand | grande | tall |
| petit | petite | short |
| jeune | jeune | young |
| vieux | vieille | old |
| gentil | gentille | kind |
| intelligent | intelligente | intelligent |
| sérieux | sérieuse | serious |
| drôle | drôle | funny |
| sympa | sympa | nice (informal) |
| timide | timide | shy |
| sportif | sportive | sporty |

Patterns: **+e**; **-eux → -euse**; **-if → -ive**; **-il → -ille**. Words already ending in *-e* don't change.
Most adjectives go **after** the noun: *une femme intelligente*. A few short common ones go before: *un petit garçon, une grande maison*. (Full rule in Week 10.)
Intensifiers: *très* (very), *assez* (quite), *un peu* (a bit), *trop* (too).

### Practice (15 min)
Make these agree:
1. Ma sœur est (sérieux) ___.  2. Mes frères sont (grand) ___.  3. Ma grand-mère est (vieux) ___ mais (sportif) ___.  4. Ta cousine est (gentil) ___.

**Check:** 1. sérieuse 2. grands 3. vieille, sportive 4. gentille

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
- Coffee Break French lessons 10–11.
- Describe 3 people you know out loud, 3 adjectives each.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Quiz
1. Conjugate *avoir* in all six persons.
2. How do you say "I'm 30" and "I'm hungry"?
3. Explain the difference in sound between *ils ont* and *ils sont*.
4. Translate "her father" and "his mother".
5. Why is it *mon amie* and not *ma amie*?
6. Say 70, 80, 90 and 99.
7. Give the feminine of *vieux, sportif, gentil, sérieux*.

**Answers:** (1) j'ai, tu as, il/elle/on a, nous avons, vous avez, ils/elles ont (2) *J'ai trente ans. J'ai faim.* (3) *ont* links with a z sound, *sont* starts with s (4) *son père*, *sa mère* (5) to avoid two vowels colliding (6) soixante-dix, quatre-vingts, quatre-vingt-dix, quatre-vingt-dix-neuf (7) vieille, sportive, gentille, sérieuse.
