**Theme:** Targeted revision, then a full DELF B1 mock exam in real conditions.
**Big question:** *Am I B1?*

## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** Diagnostic and plan
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Re-read *Mes erreurs* (all of it, the whole year).

### Lesson (20 min)
Kwiziq B1 test. Then list your scores from Weeks 47–49: writing /25, speaking /25, listening /25, reading /25.
Pick your **weakest skill** and **weakest 2 grammar points**. Tuesday–Thursday focus on them.

### Practice (15 min)
Review the grammar summaries in your notebook for your 2 weakest points; do 2 Kwiziq quizzes on each.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
innerFrench: 10 minutes.

### Log (5 min)
Log in French: your revision plan for the week.

## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** Targeted practice: weakest skill (1)
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**If listening:** 3 B1 TV5MONDE exercises, applying Week 49 strategies.
**If reading:** 2 B1 reading texts, timed, with vrai/faux + justification.
**If writing:** 1 timed essay (30 min), self-corrected with the checklist.
**If speaking:** 2 part-3 monologues (10 min prep + 5 min each), recorded.

### Practice (15 min)
Continue the same skill.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
innerFrench: 10 minutes.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Targeted practice: weakest skill (2)
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
Same as yesterday, with new material, on your weakest skill.

### Practice (15 min)
Grammar: 2 Kwiziq quizzes on your weakest points.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
10-minute Tandem voice exchange.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Second-weakest skill
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
One timed task in your second-weakest skill.

### Practice (15 min)
Correct it with the relevant checklist or answer key.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Record your part-1 answers (Week 48) once more, quickly and fluently.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Rest and readiness
**Time:** ~30 min

### Review (15 min)
Anki only.

### Lesson (10 min)
Re-read your exam-day checklist (Week 49). Prepare the paper, audio, timer and a quiet room for tomorrow.

### Log (5 min)
Log. Sleep early. You're ready.

## Day 6 — Saturday
**Topic:** Milestone: full DELF B1 mock exam
**Time:** ~3 hours

### Review (10 min)
Anki only. No studying.

### Weekly Project (150 min)
**Full DELF B1 sample paper, strictly timed:**
1. Compréhension de l'oral: ~25 min
2. Compréhension des écrits: 45 min
3. Production écrite: 45 min
*(15-minute break)*
4. Production orale: 10 min prep for part 3, then ~15 min, with a tutor as examiner or recorded alone

**Mark everything** with the official keys and grids.

### Log (15 min)
| Skill | Score /25 |
|---|---|
| Listening | |
| Reading | |
| Writing | |
| Speaking | |
| **Total /100** | |

- **50+ and no skill under 5: you're B1!** 🎉 Register for the real DELF B1 at your nearest Alliance Française.
- **Below:** don't worry. Repeat Weeks 47–49 for your weakest skill before registering. Many learners need an extra month.
