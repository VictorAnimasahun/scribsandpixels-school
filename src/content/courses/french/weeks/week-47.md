## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** The DELF B1 writing criteria
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Re-read all your *Mes erreurs* entries tagged "writing".

### Lesson (20 min)
**DELF B1 production écrite: 45 minutes, at least 160 words, /25.** Typical tasks: an opinion essay, a forum contribution, a letter/email (formal or informal) expressing and defending a point of view.

**The marking grid**
| Criterion | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Respect de la consigne | Right text type, register and length; every point in the instructions answered |
| Capacité à présenter des faits | Clearly describe events and experiences |
| Capacité à exprimer sa pensée | Give opinions, feelings and reactions with justification |
| Cohérence et cohésion | Logical order, paragraphs, connectors, pronouns (no repetition) |
| Étendue du vocabulaire | Varied words, no repetition of *chose, bien, faire* |
| Maîtrise du vocabulaire | Correct words in context |
| Morphosyntaxe | Tenses, agreements, structures (subjunctive, relatives, pronouns) |
| Orthographe | Spelling and accents |

### Practice (15 min)
Take your Week 41 and Week 43 essays and grade them against this grid, honestly, criterion by criterion. Note your 3 weakest criteria.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
innerFrench: first half of an episode.

### Log (5 min)
Log in French.

## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** The opinion essay (article / essai)
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Timed task (45 minutes):**
> *Dans votre ville, la mairie veut transformer un parc en parking. Vous écrivez un article pour le journal local pour donner votre opinion. (160 mots minimum)*

Plan for 5 minutes (intro, 2–3 arguments with examples, a concession, conclusion), write for 35, check for 5.

### Practice (15 min)
**Self-correction checklist** (use it after every text this week):
- [ ] 160+ words · title · paragraphs
- [ ] Every point of the instructions covered
- [ ] 6+ different connectors
- [ ] At least one: subjunctive · conditional · relative clause (*qui/que/dont*) · pronoun (*le/lui/y/en*)
- [ ] Agreements (adjectives, participles with *être* and preceding COD)
- [ ] Accents and spelling of 10 random words checked

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Read your essay aloud. Awkward sentences sound awkward.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** The forum post and the informal email
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Informal register**: *tu*, *Salut / Coucou*, *Bises / À plus*, a warmer tone, but still correct grammar!
**Timed task (35 minutes):**
> *Sur un forum, un internaute demande : « Je vais m'installer au Canada pour travailler. Est-ce que c'est une bonne idée d'y aller seul, sans ma famille, la première année ? » Vous répondez en racontant votre expérience ou celle d'un proche, et vous donnez votre avis. (160 mots minimum)*

### Practice (15 min)
Apply the self-correction checklist. Then rewrite your 3 weakest sentences.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Enriching your writing
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
Upgrade common "weak" words:
| Weak | Stronger options |
|---|---|
| une chose | un élément, un aspect, un point, un problème, un avantage |
| faire | réaliser, effectuer, créer, organiser, préparer |
| dire | affirmer, expliquer, souligner, préciser |
| il y a | on trouve, il existe, on compte |
| bien / bon | efficace, pertinent, bénéfique, remarquable |
| mauvais | nuisible, néfaste, décevant, inefficace |
| beaucoup de | de nombreux / nombreuses, un grand nombre de |
| très important | essentiel, primordial, crucial |
| aussi | de plus, en outre, par ailleurs |

### Practice (15 min)
Rewrite Tuesday's essay using at least 8 upgrades. Compare the two versions.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Read both versions aloud. Hear the difference.

### Log (5 min)
Log.
