## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** Looking back
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Look at your Anki stats: how many cards have you learned this year?

### Lesson (20 min)
Go through your notebook from Week 1. Find:
- your first log in French (Week 3)
- your first story in the past (Week 18)
- your first essay (Week 41)
Write a short paragraph on how your writing has changed.

### Practice (15 min)
Rewatch an Easy French episode you watched early in the year. Write 5 things you understand now that you didn't then.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
Listen to your recording diary from #1 to #7 in order. 🎧

### Log (5 min)
Log in French.

## Day 2 — Tuesday
**Topic:** Building your portfolio
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)

### Speak & Listen (10 min)

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Preparing the graduation talk
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Plan a 5-minute talk: "Mon année en français"**
1. **Where I started**: *Il y a un an, je ne savais même pas dire…* (imparfait, plus-que-parfait)
2. **The journey**: the hardest moments, the best moments, what helped (passé composé, gérondif: *en écoutant…*)
3. **What I learned beyond French**: discipline, confidence, culture
4. **What's next**: Year 2, NCLC 7, Canada (futur, futur antérieur: *D'ici un an, j'aurai…*)
5. **Advice for new learners**: *Il faut que vous… Si j'avais su, j'aurais…* (subjunctive, conditionnel passé)

Key-word notes only. No full sentences.

### Practice (15 min)
Write your key-word plan on one page.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
First run-through, timed.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 4 — Thursday
**Topic:** Rehearsal
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
Rehearse your talk 3 times. Each time, improve one thing: structure, then connectors, then pronunciation and pace.

### Practice (15 min)
Record the third run. Listen and note 3 final improvements.

### Speak & Listen (10 min)
10-minute Tandem exchange: tell your partner you're finishing your first year and ask for their advice.

### Log (5 min)
Log.

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Register and commit
**Time:** ~45 min

### Review (15 min)
Anki.

### Lesson (20 min)
**Take action:**
1. Find the next DELF B1 session at your nearest Alliance Française and note the registration deadline. Register if your Week 50 mock was a pass.
2. Put your TCF/TEF Canada target date in your calendar (Year 2 plan).
3. Tell someone about your goals. Saying it out loud makes it real.

### Log (10 min)
Log.

## Day 6 — Saturday
**Topic:** Graduation 🎓
**Time:** ~1.5 hours

### Review (10 min)
Anki. Keep your streak alive, even today.

### Weekly Project (60 min)
**Final milestone:**
1. **Record your 5-minute graduation talk** on video, looking at the camera, with notes only. This is your final recording diary entry (#8).
2. **Watch your Week 1 recording, then your graduation talk.** 🎧
3. Share the talk with someone who's been part of your journey: a Tandem partner, your tutor, family.

### Log (20 min)
**Final reflection (in French):**
- Les trois choses dont je suis le plus fier / la plus fière cette année
- Ce qui a été le plus difficile, et comment je l'ai surmonté
- Mon objectif pour l'année prochaine
- Add to Wins: **"J'ai terminé mon année de français !"** 🎉🇫🇷🇨🇦
