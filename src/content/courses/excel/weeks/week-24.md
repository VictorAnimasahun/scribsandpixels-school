## Day 1 — Monday
**Topic:** Capstone brief and plan
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Re-read your Phase 5 milestone and its README.

### Lesson (15 min)
**Capstone brief: "NaijaMart 2025–2026 Performance & Pricing Review."** The board wants one workbook that:
1. Imports and cleans all data automatically (sales 2025 + 2026 folder, products, targets, customers)
2. Shows performance vs target and year-on-year growth (H1 2026 vs H1 2025)
3. Identifies the 10 most and least profitable products, and flags products sold below a 10% margin after discounts
4. Recommends a **price change** for 3 products, with a what-if model showing the profit impact (data table or scenarios)
5. Presents everything on an interactive dashboard, plus a 1-page PDF executive summary
6. Is documented and automated (README, Checks, refresh + export button)

**Alternative:** use a real dataset from your job or Kaggle, with the same six requirements.

### Practice (20 min)
Write your plan: sheet list, queries, model tables, measures, visuals, and the order you'll build them in.

### Mini-Task (10 min)
Estimate hours per part. Block time in your calendar for Tuesday–Saturday.

Log.

## Day 3 — Wednesday
**Topic:** Build: analysis and what-if
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Run your Checks sheet: all green?

### Lesson (15 min)
A recommendation needs **evidence** (the analysis), **options** (scenarios), and **impact** (₦ figures). Structure: *"Raising the price of X by 5% would add ₦__ profit per year, assuming volume falls by at most __%."* Use a data table of price change % × volume change %.

### Practice (20 min)
Build the product profitability analysis and the pricing what-if model for 3 products.

### Mini-Task (10 min)
Write your 3 recommendations in one sentence each, with ₦ impact.

## Day 5 — Friday
**Topic:** Portfolio, CV and interviews
**Time:** ~1 hour

### Review (10 min)
Final Checks run on the capstone.

### Lesson (15 min)
**Portfolio** (a Google Drive/OneDrive folder or a simple website): 5 best pieces, each with a screenshot, a 2-line description, and the file or a 1-minute video: capstone · executive dashboard · automated ETL · invoice + payroll · custom function library.
**CV bullets** (results, not duties):
- *Built an automated Power Query + Power Pivot reporting model that reduced monthly report preparation from 6 hours to 5 minutes.*
- *Designed an interactive sales dashboard tracking ₦830M+ in annual sales across 9 stores.*
- *Cleaned and standardised a 300-record customer database (phones, names, locations) with Power Query.*
**Interview Excel tests** usually include: VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, a PivotTable, IF logic, a chart, sometimes data cleaning. You've done all of them. Practise talking while doing: *"I'll convert this to a table first so the formulas expand…"*

### Practice (20 min)
Assemble your portfolio folder and write 4 CV bullets about your Excel skills.

### Mini-Task (10 min)
Update LinkedIn: headline, skills (Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, VBA, dashboards), and a featured link to your portfolio.

### Log (5 min)
Log.
